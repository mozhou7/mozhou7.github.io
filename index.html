About Me

Hi, I'm Mo. I am a postdoctoral scholar at the Institute for Foundations of Data Science (IFDS) and the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington, hosted by Simon Du and Maryam Fazel.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Duke University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Rong Ge. Before that, I received my B.S. in Statistics from Peking University.

I have also spent time at Stanford with Tengyu Ma (summer 2023), at AWS AI with Erran Li (summer 2022), and at Georgia Tech with Tuo Zhao (summer 2018).

Email | Google Scholar

Research

My research focuses on the optimization and mathematical foundations of modern machine learning, especially large-scale overparameterized models. I study how loss landscapes, training dynamics, and optimizer design shape the behavior of these models.

A central question motivating my research is: Why do simple algorithms and ideas often work surprisingly well in practice, and what fundamental principles are behind it?
